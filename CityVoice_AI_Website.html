Responsible AI Notice

Fairness
Classification is tested across varied phrasing so no neighbourhood or complaint style is deprioritised.
Transparency
Citizens are told their complaint was triaged by AI and may request human review.
Ethics
AI routing is advisory to municipal staff, not a final automated decision.
Privacy
Complaint data is used only for resolution — nothing is stored beyond what's needed.
CityVoice AI — a project prototype. Not an official government service.
